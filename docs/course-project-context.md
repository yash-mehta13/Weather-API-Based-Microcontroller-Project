# Course Project Context

The project relates to **BECE204L Microprocessors and Microcontrollers** (Winter Semester 2024-25), whose course-project brief asks teams to design and build a hardware prototype of an embedded system on a board such as 8051/Arduino/Raspberry Pi/STM/ESP, with themes aligned to or supporting the Sustainable Development Goals. The brief describes Review 1 (topic, problem survey, processor rationale), Review 2 (progress) and Review 3 (functional model), with evaluation on individual contribution, originality and innovation.

## Intended SDG relevance
Weather-aware irrigation assistance may relate to responsible water use and agriculture, potentially connecting to **SDG 6 (Clean Water and Sanitation)** and **SDG 2 (Zero Hunger)**. This is intended relevance only: no water savings or yield effects have been measured, and the project has not been certified or independently verified against any SDG.

This repository does not record team members, marks, review outcomes or experimental results.
